Services

Three layers. Pricing you can see.

The free Durability Check is the entry point — an instant, self-serve read. The paid work is human-reviewed: the blueprint is the productized engagement, and custom advisory is for organizations whose governance is load-bearing enough to justify implementation work, not just a framework document.

Tier 1

AI Governance Durability Check

Free

Self-serve. Result on the spot.

A self-assessment that estimates how long your current AI governance approach will last before it breaks. Structured around the eight structural causes that documented governance failures keep tracing back to.

You get a durability band — Brittle, Aging, or Durable-ish — set by your weakest structural cause, with the specific sections that set the expiration date, the first trigger that would void a control, and a 90-day plan. Every part of the result traces back to an answer you gave. Because it is self-reported, it is an estimate, not an audit — but if you never pay me a dollar, it still tells you something you didn't have and couldn't easily generate yourself.

Every Tier 2 includes

  1. Risk and Impact Map

    Inventory of AI systems, decisions affected, populations affected, and the structural risks each carries — not a matrix exercise, a deployment-aware document.

  2. Decision-Process RACI with shelf-life annex

    Who decides, who consults, with what information, under what escalation. Every role paired with a named person. Every section ends with "this section expires when…"

  3. Governance Framework Document

    25–40 pages, decision-process focused. Names what triggers a re-review, who owns each trigger, and what the escalation looks like under pressure.

  4. Implementation Roadmap

    Four phases, capacity-aware. Sequenced so the highest-impact, lowest-effort items ship first; the rest is staged against staffing and budget reality.

  5. $1,500 credit toward Tier 3

    If you book a Tier 3 engagement within 60 days, the entire Tier 2 fee credits up to $1,500.

How to choose

A short decision tree.

  • Want to know whether this even applies to you?

    → The free Durability Check.

  • The check surfaced structural gaps. You want a defensible blueprint?

    → Tier 2 (sub-tier by org size and sector).

  • You need someone to do the implementation, not just write the framework?

    → Tier 3 Implementation or Full Program.

  • You want continuity across model updates and regulatory shifts?

    → Fractional retainer.

  • You're a foundation or municipality with RFP-bound procurement?

    → Tier 3 public sector; vehicle scoped per engagement.

  • Not sure where to start?

    → The free Durability Check. It's substantive, and it tells me what kind of engagement (if any) makes sense.
