Engagement scope

What I do not consult on.

Standing policy, not case-by-case judgment. The work this practice is set up to do does not extend to:

  • Trading theses
  • Market-timing
  • Earnings-call interpretation
  • Public-company equity assessments
  • Surveillance technology
  • Predictive policing
  • Credit-scoring or insurance-risk scoring
  • Law-enforcement analytics not framed as reform

Primary audience: public-sector programs, foundations, academic institutions, civic-tech organizations, and enterprise AI governance initiatives.

Expert-network calls

For one-off questions you can route through OnFrontiers, NewtonX, or Catalant. Direct calls are $550+/hr; routed calls go at network rates. The direct rate sits above the network-net rate by design — calling me directly buys access, not a discount. Booking through a network is fine; the engagement scope above still applies.
