The Governance Shelf-Life Diagnostic™

If you never pay me a dollar, this report still gives you information you didn't have and couldn't easily generate yourself. That's the commitment.

The diagnostic estimates how long your current AI governance approach will last before it breaks. Ten questions, structured around the dimensions that documented governance failures keep tracing back to: external reference stability, accountability locus, feedback paths, scope drift, institutional memory, update triggers, stakeholder deliberation, dissent handling, endogenous drift, and vendor dependency.

It's not a score. A number reduces a structural analysis to a metric and invites gaming. You'll get a shelf-life range in months, a three-paragraph narrative naming which structural gaps set the expiration date, and a sketch of what durability would look like for an organization like yours.

Form coming online

The form is being finalized. While it spins up, you can take the diagnostic by email — send a note to chris@graziul.io with a one-line description of your organization (sector, rough headcount, what AI systems are in use) and I'll reply with the questionnaire.

What happens next

  1. Seven to ten business days. No instant report.

    Each one is human-reviewed. The delay is the quality signal, not an apology.

  2. No upsells in the email.

    The only follow-up is the report itself. If your shelf-life estimate is critical and the cause is structural, I'll flag a recommended next step. You decide whether to pursue.

  3. Email retained until you ask to delete.

    No newsletter signup, no marketing list. See the privacy notice .
